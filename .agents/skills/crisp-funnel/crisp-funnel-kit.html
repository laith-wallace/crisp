SECTION 02

Qualifier

R — ResponsiveI — Intelligent

Single-select cards. The tap selects and advances with no continue button. Each answer is data the funnel uses later to personalise the result. The interaction is the engagement, and the engagement is the lead quality.

Converts
Tap-to-advance feels instant and effortless. One decision per screen beats a long form.
Use when
Early in the funnel to qualify and to start the personalisation thread. Two to four steps, no more.
CRISP test
Does the card respond inside 100ms, and does the answer change what comes next?

SECTION 03

Multi-select

R — ResponsiveI — Intelligent

For questions with more than one valid answer. The continue button stays disabled until a choice is made, then activates to lime. The button label reports the state so the visitor is never guessing what unlocks progress.

Converts
A state-reporting button removes the dead tap. The visitor sees exactly when they can proceed.
Use when
The answer is genuinely multiple. Do not use it for single-choice questions, it adds a step.
CRISP test
Does the button change state on selection, not on submit?

SECTION 04

Value / belief step

I — Intelligent

A single insight placed between questions. It raises belief before the offer lands and gives the visitor a reason the funnel exists. One stat, one claim, one line. Never a wall of education.

Converts
Belief built before the ask lifts opt-in. The lone stat earns the next tap.
Use when
The audience is problem-aware but not solution-aware. Skip it for warm, ready-to-buy traffic.
CRISP test
Is this a single insight, or raw information the visitor must process?

SECTION 05

Social proof

I — Intelligent

One specific result at the decision point. A named person, a problem before, a number after. Vague praise does nothing. The strongest proof is a concrete outcome the visitor wants for themselves.

Converts
Proof adjacent to the CTA reduces drop-off at the moment of decision.
Use when
Just before the offer or the form. One quote, not a carousel.

CRISP positions against AI-generated filler. Never ship an invented testimonial here. Leave the placeholder until a real beta audit result is in hand. A fabricated quote undercuts the whole product.

SECTION 06

Loader / processing

R — ResponsiveI — Intelligent

A short, determinate wait that makes the result feel computed, not canned. The cycling status lines name real work being done. Perceived effort raises the perceived value of what arrives next.

Converts
A worked result feels personal. The wait builds anticipation rather than friction, if kept under four seconds.
Use when
Right before a personalised result or offer. Never fake a long delay, it reads as a trick.
CRISP test
Is the bar determinate, and does it complete inside the time the visitor will wait?

SECTION 07

Offer

C — ContextualP — Powerful

The pitch. What they get, in three concrete lines, then one action. Depth is hidden here on purpose. The novice sees a clean offer, the detail lives one tap deeper for those who want it.

Converts
Three specific deliverables beat a paragraph of claims. The single CTA carries all the visual weight.
Use when
After value and proof, before the form. State the offer, do not sell past the close.
CRISP test
Is there one obvious action, and is complexity held back from the first-time visitor?

SECTION 08

Capture form

R — ResponsiveS — Seamless

Three fields, no more, for a first touch. Validation fires on blur, not on submit, so the visitor is corrected before they reach the button. Autocomplete is on every field so the device does the typing.

Converts
Fewer fields plus inline validation plus autofill removes the friction that kills mobile form completion.
Use when
The single capture point. Earn each field, drop any the audit does not need.
CRISP test
Does validation fire on blur, and is autocomplete set on every field?

SECTION 09

Booking

S — Seamless

Slot selection in the funnel, not a redirect to a separate calendar tool. The visitor stays in the flow they started. The booking fits their day rather than forcing them into yours.

Converts
In-funnel booking removes the portal redirect that loses warm leads at the handoff.
Use when
High-ticket offers that need a call. For self-serve, skip straight to confirmation.
CRISP test
Can they book without leaving the funnel or logging in?

SECTION 10

Confirmation

C — ContextualS — Seamless

The close. It confirms the action landed, restates what was booked, and names the next step in plain terms. No dead end. The visitor leaves knowing exactly what happens and when, then returns to their day.

Converts
A clear post-action state cuts the "did that work" support reply and sets up the next touch.
Use when
Every funnel ends here. Always state what happens next, never leave a thank-you with no path.
CRISP test
Does the visitor know the action worked and what to expect next?
