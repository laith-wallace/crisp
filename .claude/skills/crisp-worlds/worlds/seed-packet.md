## Layout grammar

Front and back. The top of each page is the front of the packet: one illustration in a printed frame, a name in a warm serif and a single line of promise. Below is the back: a practical grid of facts (when, how deep, how long, how many) in small labelled cells.

## Signature moves

1. A framed illustration panel with a thin rule border and a name plate below it.
2. A facts grid of small labelled cells (Sow, Harvest, Spacing) that turns instructions into a scannable table.
3. Earthy fills (tomato, mustard, leaf) used in blocks, with leaf green reserved for the action.

## Avoid

- Stock photography. Illustration or a single honest product photo.
- Rustic clichés: burlap textures, chalkboards, script fonts.
- Hiding the practical facts in paragraphs.

## In product UI

Brand register first: food, garden, wellness, craft and education. In a product, the facts grid is the reusable idea; a recipe, plan or subscription detail page can carry the front-and-back structure without the illustration.
