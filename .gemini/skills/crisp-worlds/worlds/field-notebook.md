## Layout grammar

A narrow margin column on the left holds dates, tags and short annotations; the main column holds the entries. Pale ruled lines set the baseline rhythm. Headings are written, not shouted: medium size, bold serif, flush left.

## Signature moves

1. A red margin rule down the left edge, with dates and status written in the margin beside each entry.
2. Data labels and coordinates in a typewriter mono, as if stamped in the field.
3. Pencil blue for actions and links, the colour of a note added later.

## Avoid

- Fake handwriting fonts. The notebook lives in the structure, not in a script face.
- Torn paper edges, tape and doodles. One borrowed idea, not a costume.
- Dense tables without the margin, because the margin is what makes it a notebook.

## In product UI

A dashboard becomes a log: dated entries in the main column with status and owner in the margin. Settings read as a short ruled form, one field per line, with help text in the margin rather than in tooltips. Fits research tools, CRMs, inspection apps, journaling and anything where history matters.
