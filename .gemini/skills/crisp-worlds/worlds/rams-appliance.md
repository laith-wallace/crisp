## Layout grammar

A calm, symmetrical-feeling grid of controls with generous but even spacing, grouped by function. Perfect circles for round controls, rounded rectangles for panels, and a strict alignment of every label to its control. Grey does almost everything.

## Signature moves

1. One orange control per screen: the thing that turns it on, starts it or saves it.
2. Lowercase, short labels set beside or below controls in small grey type, never inside.
3. Grids of perfect circles (dots, dials, status lights) that make the layout feel engineered.

## Avoid

- More than one accent; the single coloured switch is the identity.
- Decoration of any kind: gradients, illustrations, patterns.
- Dense tables; this world prefers fewer, clearer controls.

## In product UI

Settings screens, device control apps, media players, smart-home and consumer hardware companions. A settings page is a calm panel of grouped switches with the one orange "save" or "power". Dashboards show a few large, labelled readings in a grid.
