# Brief: Ferro Coffee

- A small cafe on Calle de Embajadores, Lavapies, Madrid.
- Open Tuesday to Sunday, 8:00 to 16:00. Closed Monday.
- Coffee from one roaster, Hola Coffee, changes each month.
- Menu: cortado 2.20 EUR, flat white 3.20 EUR, filter 3.00 EUR, toast with tomato 3.50 EUR, cardamom bun 2.80 EUR.
- 14 seats inside, a bench outside.
- No reservations. Card or cash.
- Main action: get directions.
