# Brief: Equipment check-out for Hale Rowing Club

- Members borrow club boats and oars.
- Boats have names (for example "Ada", "Severn") and a class: single scull (1x), double (2x), coxless four (4-), eight (8+).
- A member books a boat for a slot of 90 minutes, between 6:00 and 20:00.
- Boats can be marked "out of service" by the boatman.
- 22 boats, about 140 members.
- Main action: book a boat.
