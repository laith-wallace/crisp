# Draft: Miga Bakery

Sourdough. Done slowly.

We bake honestly, the way bread used to be made. Rather than chasing trends, we focus on three things: flour, water, time.

48 hours | 3 ingredients | 1 oven

It started in 2019, when Priya found an old bakery on the corner and kept the original tiles.

Freshness isn't a slogan here. It's a habit.

No shortcuts, no additives, no compromise.

Come early. The cardamom buns go first. They always do.
