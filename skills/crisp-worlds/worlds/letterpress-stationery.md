## Layout grammar

Wide margins and a centred, narrow measure, like a letter. A small crest or monogram at the top, generous space, then content in short blocks separated by hairline rules. Calls to action are text links with a rule beneath, or one bottle-green button.

## Signature moves

1. Wide letter margins with a narrow centred column, and nothing touching the edges.
2. A small debossed crest or monogram with a thin foil-gold rule, the only metallic touch.
3. Bottle-green ink for type and the single action, instead of black.

## Avoid

- Saturated accents and badges; luxury is quiet.
- Gold text (it fails contrast); foil is for rules and the crest only.
- Long scrolling feature lists. Say less.

## In product UI

Brand register: hospitality, private banking, property, high-ticket services and invitations. In an app, it fits the booking confirmation, the itinerary and account statements; keep forms plain with the green action.
